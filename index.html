About Me

I am interested in understanding the physics of compact objects, particularly accreting neutron stars and thermonuclear X-ray bursts. My work focuses on timing, spectral, and polarimetric studies using data from modern X-ray observatories such as XMM-Newton and IXPE. I am passionate about observational astronomy, data analysis, and teaching physics.

Research Interests

Thermonuclear X-ray Bursts

Investigating unstable nuclear burning on neutron star surfaces.

Neutron Star Physics

Photospheres, atmospheres, equation of state, and accretion processes.

XMM-Newton Analysis

Timing and spectral analysis of compact objects.

IXPE Polarimetry

Exploring polarization signatures in high-energy sources.

Accretion Physics

Disk dynamics and energy release around compact objects.

High-Energy Astrophysics

Observational studies of extreme astrophysical environments.
