About Me

I am interested in understanding the physics of compact objects, particularly accreting neutron stars and thermonuclear X-ray bursts. My work focuses on timing, spectral, and polarimetric studies using data from modern X-ray observatories such as XMM-Newton and IXPE. I am passionate about observational astronomy, data analysis, and teaching physics.

Research Interests
